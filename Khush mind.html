KHUSH'S 12-WEEK PROGRAM

Build confidence, stay composed under pressure, and perform more consistently in matches. One session a week, ten minutes a day.

How it works

Five pieces, repeated every week.

Weekly session45–60 min, same day each week. Review, learn, practise, commit.
Daily check-in2 min on WhatsApp every evening by 9pm.
Daily habits10 min across morning and night.
Pre-game routine15 min on every match day.
Post-game review15 min within 24 hours of every match.

EACH WEEKLY SESSION

  1. Review (10 min) — last week's tracker, check-ins and match reviews.
  2. Learn (15 min) — this week's theme and why it matters.
  3. Practise (15 min) — do the exercise together, out loud.
  4. Commit (5 min) — one homework task and one cricket goal.

The 12 weeks

Tap a week to open it.

WEEKS 1–4 · FOUNDATIONS
01Goal setting›

Exercise: Write one season goal, three monthly goals and weekly process goals — things you control, not scores.

Homework: Read your goals every morning.

02Belief audit›

Exercise: List 10 beliefs about your cricket. Mark each one helpful or limiting.

Homework: Pick the 3 most limiting beliefs.

03Belief modification›

Exercise: Rewrite each limiting belief using evidence against it. "I always nick off early" becomes "I've survived the first 10 balls plenty of times."

Homework: Read your new beliefs every night.

04Routine & habits›

Exercise: Design a morning and night routine. Start the habit tracker.

Homework: Hit 5 out of 7 days on the tracker.

WEEKS 5–8 · SKILLS
05Visualisation›

Exercise: 10-minute guided script — walk out, take guard, play three perfect shots or bowl three perfect balls. See, hear and feel every detail.

Homework: Visualise for 5–10 minutes daily.

06Self-talk & affirmations›

Exercise: Catch negative self-talk. Build 5 personal affirmations and 3 cue words ("watch it", "next ball", "breathe").

Homework: Say your affirmations each morning.

07Focus & concentration›

Exercise: Switch off between balls, switch on with a trigger — tap the bat, look at the sightscreen.

Homework: Use your trigger in every net session.

08Confidence building›

Exercise: Build a "confidence bank" — your 20 best cricket moments, written in detail.

Homework: Add one new deposit every week.

WEEKS 9–12 · PERFORMING UNDER PRESSURE
09Pressure management›

Exercise: Box breathing (in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4). Simulate pressure in the nets: "6 needed off the last over."

Homework: Box breathing before bed.

10Pre-game routine›

Exercise: Rehearse the full pre-game routine until it's automatic.

Homework: Run it at every match.

11Handling failure›

Exercise: The 3-step reset — accept it, learn one thing, let it go. Review a bad dismissal calmly.

Homework: Post-game review after every match.

12Gratitude & review›

Exercise: Three things you're grateful for in cricket. Compare your week 1 goals and beliefs to now.

Homework: Set goals for the next 12 weeks.

Pre-game routine

Same steps, every match day.

  1. Night before — pack kit, 5-min visualisation, read goals, phone off 30 min before bed.
  2. Morning — affirmations and one process goal for today.
  3. At the ground — walk the pitch, check conditions, 2 min box breathing.
  4. Before batting or bowling — 3 deep breaths, cue word, picture the first ball going well.
  5. Between balls — switch off, then trigger to switch on. One ball at a time.
  6. After a mistake — breathe, "next ball", reset. Review it later, not now.

Post-game review

Fill this in within 24 hours, then copy it to WhatsApp.

Daily habits

About 10 minutes a day.

  • Read goals and affirmations (2 min)
  • Visualisation, 5–10 min (from week 5)
  • 3 rounds of box breathing (2 min)
  • Write 3 good things from the day (2 min)
  • Phone off 30 min before bed
